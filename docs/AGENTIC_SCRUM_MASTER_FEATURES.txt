Generated: 2026-03-25

1) Product Overview
Agentic Scrum Master is a multi-tenant agile delivery platform focused on sprint planning, execution, and reporting. It combines AI-assisted operations, project governance, team collaboration, and engineering integrations in one workspace.

Core goals:
- Plan work with clear goals, backlog structure, and sprint scope.
- Execute and monitor work across board, tasks, assignments, and standups.
- Keep delivery healthy with reporting, risk detection, and automation.
- Connect engineering systems (GitHub, webhooks, integrations).
- Support admins, scrum masters, developers, and org owners.
