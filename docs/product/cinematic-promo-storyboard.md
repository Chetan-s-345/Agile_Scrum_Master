# Agile Scrum Master Cinematic Promo Storyboard

## Purpose

Create a premium, cinematic launch promo for Agile Scrum Master that feels emotional first, product second.

## Core Idea

From sprint chaos to team clarity.

The story follows a team lead who is carrying too much invisible coordination work. The product enters as a turning point: not another dashboard, but a force multiplier that gives the team rhythm, confidence, and momentum.

## Audience

- Engineering managers
- Scrum masters
- Startup founders
- Product leaders managing execution velocity

## Brand Tone

- Cinematic and ambitious
- Human and grounded
- Confident, never overhyped
- Fast-paced but clear

## Final Deliverables

- Hero cut: 75 to 90 seconds
- Social cut: 30 seconds
- Teaser cut: 12 seconds

## Narrative Arc

1. Problem world: fragmented tools, context switching, missed signals.
2. Emotional cost: late nights, uncertainty, team fatigue.
3. Turning point: Agile Scrum Master centralizes flow and decisions.
4. Transformation: visible sprint health, actionable reports, better collaboration.
5. Outcome: confident shipping cadence.

## Main Character Backstory

Aditi is a high-performing engineering lead in a fast-growing product team. She used to write code daily, but now spends most of her week unblocking planning, chasing updates, and fixing communication gaps between GitHub, sprint boards, and meetings.

She is not burned out because she is weak. She is burned out because the system around her is fragmented.

When she adopts Agile Scrum Master, she does not become superhuman. Her team simply regains focus because planning, assignment, monitoring, and reporting become one connected operating loop.

## Cinematography Direction

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 for hero, 9:16 reframes for social.
- Lens language: start with tight close-ups and handheld tension, shift to stable wider frames after product reveal.
- Camera movement: push-ins during stress scenes, smooth sliders and gentle dolly moves during solution scenes.
- Lighting: cool low-key tones in problem scenes, neutral and warm tones in transformation scenes.
- Color transition: desaturated early scenes to richer, cleaner contrast after turning point.

## Editing Style

- First act: fast cuts, layered UI overlays, brief intentional glitch transitions.
- Middle act: match-cut from chaos interfaces into clean product UI.
- Final act: longer shots, breathing room, confidence pacing.

## Sound Design

- Intro: soft ambient rumble, notification textures, subtle heartbeat pulse.
- Transition: low cinematic rise and impact sweep.
- Solution: uplifting hybrid score with light percussion and piano motif.
- Outro: resolved chord with short mnemonic sting.
